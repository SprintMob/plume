# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be someone the other people here would want to work with again. Assume the
person you are replying to is competent and busy.

## The specific version

This project exists because 25 pull requests sat unread for four months. Whatever
else happens here, that will not be the experience of anyone who opens one. A
patch gets an answer, and if the answer is no it comes with a reason.

Not acceptable, and enforced: harassment of any kind, personal attacks,
sustained disruption, publishing someone's private information, and unwelcome
attention after being asked to stop. Deciding someone's work is bad is fine.
Deciding someone is bad is not.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, in issues and pull requests, and anywhere
someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Until `conduct@sprintmob.com` is active, send a report through
[GitHub's private reporting channel](https://github.com/SprintMob/plume/security/advisories)
with **Conduct report** at the start of the title. Reports stay between you and
the maintainers. Responses run from a private word through to a permanent ban,
and whoever handles a report will say which one they chose and why.

Maintainers who do not hold to this are held to it harder, not less.
